# Theos Trofi

Website for Theos Trofi — fresh juice in every cup.

## Put it online with GitHub Pages

1. Create a new repository on GitHub (for example `theos-trofi`).
2. Upload everything in this folder: `index.html`, `favicon.svg`, `README.md` and the `images` folder.
3. In the repository, open **Settings → Pages**.
4. Under **Branch**, choose `main` and `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
5. After a minute or two your site is live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/theos-trofi/`.

## Before going live, replace the placeholders in `index.html`

- Branch addresses, delivery area and opening hours (search for `[Street address]`)
- Phone numbers and email (search for `09XX` and `your@email.com`)
- Drink prices (the `DRINKS` list near the bottom of the file)
- The sample reviews (search for `Sample review`)

## Orders

Customers fill in their name, address, contact number, note and payment method,
then copy the order summary and send it to Theos Trofi on Facebook Messenger.
